How to play

In Roguelike Run, win the most bouts and draft a skill after each one. You have 30 seconds, and the next bout starts when both players lock in. The first card is picked automatically at zero. Classic Duel is first to three wins. Lock-on keeps your rival in view. Drag to aim manually.

In Open Meadow, both players have to be inside the gold ring for attacks to deal damage. View chat with T, or type instantly with Enter, and open emotes with B.

Move WASD
Strafe or close the gap. Use the left joystick on touch.
Slap J or click
Chain three close-range slaps to launch your rival. Time a slap to send their spit back.
Spit E or right-click
Hold and release for a larger, faster glob. At full charge it tracks rivals. Dodge or slap it back.
Dodge Shift
Slip past a hit at the last second to power up your next slap.
Nova Q
When the gold meter fills, unleash a shockwave up close.
